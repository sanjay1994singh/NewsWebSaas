Yes. Features, limits, add-ons, and tenant overrides are controlled in Django Admin.

Does payment publish a site?

No. Subscription, onboarding, review, and publication are separate states.

Is AdSense approval guaranteed?

No. Press Nexa provides AdSense integration support only.
